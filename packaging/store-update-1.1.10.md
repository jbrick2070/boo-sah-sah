# Microsoft Store release notes for version 1.1.10.0

The "What's new in this version" field in each language: this paragraph first, then the 1.1.9.0 paragraph from `packaging/store-update-1.1.9.md`, separated by a blank line. Only what the Store build has.

## en-US

New: street life. Taipei's streets are now lined with trees and lamps, groves fill the open ground between the blocks, and the rooftops carry gardens, water tanks and lit signs. Shopfronts glow at the foot of the towers at night, and over the open city a lower moon picks out the towers' faces.
