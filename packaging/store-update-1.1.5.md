# Microsoft Store release notes for version 1.1.5.0

## en-US

Circle Taipei 101, follow black kites above the rooftops and watch a delivery drone lower its parcel. The new Real-time graphics panel in the upper-left shows which features are active on your PC. Click a row to change the setting during the film.
