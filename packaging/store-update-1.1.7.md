# Microsoft Store release notes for version 1.1.7.0

The whole "What's new in this version" field in each language (it replaces the 1.1.5.0 and 1.1.6.0 notes). Only what the Store build has: the PhysX genres on the dial and the PhysX Explore marbles are test-rig features and are not mentioned.

## en-US

New: a Workout dial (Shift+Up and Shift+Down) fills the sky with up to 200,000 ray-traced birds to load your GPU, while keeping the frame rate above 24 fps. The dashboard shows your GPU's temperature, power and clock. A Full screen button sits in the top corner, and Esc leaves full screen. The loading bar shows real progress and checks every file. Some of the demo's motion is now simulated with NVIDIA PhysX: the parcel swings on its line, Madame N's iris turns as a geared mechanism, Taipei 101's damper sways, and the canopy ribbons billow as cloth.
