# Microsoft Store release notes for version 1.1.8.0

The "What's new in this version" field in each language: this paragraph first, then the 1.1.7.0 paragraph from `packaging/store-update-1.1.7.md`, separated by a blank line. Only what the Store build has.

## en-US

New: the demo now opens with "Last Light", 45 seconds at dusk over Taipei. The sunset turns the reflecting pool to gold, a silk banner streams in the wind (cloth simulated with NVIDIA PhysX), and a kettle of black kites circles Taipei 101 as the sun goes down and the city's lights come on. While the simulated cloth plays, the dashboard's PhysX row reads BAKED CLOTH.
