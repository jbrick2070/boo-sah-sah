# BOO SAH SAH graphics update plan

## What changed in the 1.1.5 candidate

The six-minute film now makes two complete camera orbits. The first follows the Glass Orchid petals past a mirror. The second holds Taipei 101 in frame as the skyline and surveyed basin terrain pass behind it. Between the orbits, the river patrol, delivery winch, black kites and airborne refueling pair give the camera subjects with visible movement. Reduced Motion shortens both arcs. [Film shot list](film_shot_list.md)

At 1:35 in the film, a 2560×1440 run on the RTX 5080 Laptop GPU measured 15.51 ms median GPU frame time at native resolution, 10.67 ms with DLSS Super Resolution Quality, and 13.86 ms with Quality Ray Reconstruction. The corresponding 95th percentiles were 16.42, 11.88 and 15.21 ms over 60 samples per mode. The Quality input was 1707×960. Super Resolution used 31% less median GPU time in this shot. The benchmark held the driver's background profiling constant for repeatable captures.

At 1920×1080 with fixed 6× Frame Generation and VSync off, the final half-second measurement in a separate 45-second run was 69.7 frames rendered and 418.2 presented per second. NVIDIA's counter includes frames submitted to Windows above the monitor's refresh rate. The upper-left panel reports both numbers. NPU workload uses its own inference counter and rate. [Dashboard controls](USER_GUIDE.md)

The release benchmark reproduced the same 10-bit pixels after each of 49 seeks. Its four suites covered 13 graphics configurations, 18 authored film shots, 15 forced-raster shots and three native fallback cases with both NVIDIA reconstruction DLLs absent.

## The 1.1 release

Keep the separate 180-second trilogy useful as a daylight and night comparison. Its 75 s and 108 s shots share the asset with the full film’s 260 s shot, but have different cameras and lighting. ## Next graphics experiments in priority order

1. **Stabilize edges in motion.** First prototype per-part previous transforms for the airframe, rotor blades and orchid petals. Check the resulting motion vectors against a known camera pan and a stationary camera with the rotors spinning. Then compare live NRD REBLUR with a history-clamped temporal resolve on thin silhouettes. Inspect disocclusion at blade tips and clear history on seeks, cuts, edition or preset changes, and device recreation. Preserve the stateless path for reproducible captures.
2. **Refine the street-level city and the strange reflections.** Test local shop interiors and rain-slick curb reflections in a slower street-height camera pass. The shipped city covers the film’s flight paths. Continuous streaming for unrestricted long-distance exploration would be a separate project. For the intercept, prototype a delayed chromatic reflection confined to the water or terminal glass. Vary it with the soundtrack and test silent playback and reverse seeks. Prototype a low-resolution, depth-filtered volumetric grid for directional shafts if the flight shot would benefit from searchlight beams. Evaluate brighter practical lights and a wider bloom chain against the Dawn finale before adopting either globally.
3. **Evaluate DLSS and HDR10 as separate display experiments.** A Streamline DLAA or DLSS reconstruction trial needs correct motion vectors, camera jitter, depth and exposure, along with a verified runtime path on supported NVIDIA hardware. Compare it against the native image in motion and record frame time at equal output resolution. An HDR10 trial separately needs display capability detection, output color space, transfer function, display calibration and SDR fallback. Validate the clean capture, codec inset and UI on SDR and HDR displays. The existing 10-bit internal canvas alone establishes neither HDR10 output nor DLSS support.

## Verified release candidate

- The software-only WARP driver now selects a lightweight diagnostic animation that follows the film clock and edition. The old full-film path stalled and crashed the software driver on teardown at 258 seconds. The corrected Traditional Chinese regression rendered 471 frames in four seconds and exited normally. A separate 15-second repeat presented 1,507 frames. An Arabic Midnight run crossed the 275-second transition and also exited normally. Physical GPUs continue to use the full scene. [WARP regression](../tests/integration/warp_playback.cmake) · [Software renderer diagnostics](../build/x64-release/warp-test/diagnostics.json)
- All 23 unit tests and 19 integration tests passed. The 12 effects-detail renders repeated exactly after seeking, and reduced-flash playback suppressed the injected beat. On a hardware GPU with ray tracing, CUDA, Tensor and the NPU disabled, the Traditional Chinese Safe-mode run completed 896 frames with no effect faults. Requesting High detail correctly selected Low under Safe.
