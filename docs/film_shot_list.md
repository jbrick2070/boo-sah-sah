# The Glass Orchid Protocol — six-minute film shot list

Times are elapsed from the start of the full film with the film camera active. The two full turns have different subjects. The first stays close enough to see the orchid petals overlap. The second keeps Taipei 101 in frame while the city moves behind it.

| Time | Subject | Camera and action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:28 | Taipei 101 observatory and Xinyi skyline | Open near the tower's tuned mass damper. Drift out over the rooflines and widen the view across the basin, then descend toward the sanctuary terrace. |
| 0:28–0:45 | Rooftop sanctuary | Settle at flower height as the orchid opens and the enamel arches rise around it. |
| 0:45–1:22 | Glass Orchid and mirror stelae | Circle the bloom from 0:46 to 1:20. As the camera rounds its side, pan along the bronze frame to the dark mirror and hold on its face at 0:59.5. Bring the flower back into frame by 1:11. |
| 1:22–1:30 | Shanghai river patrol | Cut to the bridge deck. Move forward past the near rail and pan partway with the police helicopter so its movement remains visible against the Bund. |
| 1:30–1:40 | Return to the orchid | Pull back from the bloom to show more of the terrace as the camera drops toward the reflecting pool. |
| 1:40–2:10 | Neural Velvet reflecting pool | Track sideways along the near lip of the pool and tilt up from the reflected light through the flower. |
| 2:10–2:20 | Woven canopy detail | Cut close to a single illuminated ribbon and travel up its curve. Its edge leads the eye to the crown of the arch. |
| 2:20–2:30 | Canopy and bloom | Cut wide from the ribbon to show the flower beneath it and finish the move across the pool. |
| 2:30–2:47 | Madame N's iris | Drift sideways across the brass iris, holding its opening near the center of the frame. |
| 2:47–3:15 | Parcel winch and sanctuary ecology | Cut to the north parapet for the delivery. Lower the camera with the case and hold on its latch as the planting cue fires at 2:55. At 2:59 cut back to the terrace, then descend toward the open iris and its planting. |
| 3:15–3:33.5 | Particle Mutiny | Track across the open south side of the sanctuary for a side view of the turning strands. The CUDA formation alternates between a helix and a ring. |
| 3:33.5–3:52 | Particle crown | Start the crane while the sideways track finishes at 3:35.4. Rise as the strands curl into a ring. Near 3:44.4 the angle reveals its open center. |
| 3:52–4:05 | Black kites over Taipei | Follow the wingbeats as the birds bank across the rooftops. The turning flock echoes the circling particles. At 4:01, cut back to the sanctuary before the flight over Shenzhen. |
| 4:05–4:35 | Shenzhen Bay aircraft | Follow the banking eVTOL across its nose toward a starboard view. From 4:21.5 to 4:31.5, tilt up and tighten on the airborne refueling pair, then return to the landing pad for touchdown. |
| 4:35–4:55 | Taipei terrace at night | Return to a closer, gently moving view across the orchid and illuminated sanctuary before leaving the city. |
| 4:55–5:01 | Space station above Earth | Cut to the station and its solar arrays against Earth's curved horizon. A slight drift separates the station from the planet. |
| 5:01–5:31 | Taipei 101 crown and basin | Circle the tower once. Tighten on the observation-deck windows from 5:03, then widen from 5:11 to bring the ridgelines back into view. Pause briefly on the returning skyline. |
| 5:31–5:42 | Terrace to dawn bathroom | Return to the orchid. At 5:35.5 the camera moves into the penthouse as the flower resolves into a keepsake in the vanity vase. |
| 5:42–6:00 | Dawn end card | Pull back for ten seconds to include the vanity, mirror and Taipei 101 window. Settle into a near-still title composition for the final eight seconds. |

With **Reduced Motion** on, the orchid camera moves about ten degrees to either side of its opening angle. The tower camera covers about fifteen degrees. The parcel camera stays closer to the dock, the particle crane travels about one eighth of its full distance, and the space capsule appears already docked. With CUDA off, the Particle Mutiny camera frames the GPU fallback ring.
