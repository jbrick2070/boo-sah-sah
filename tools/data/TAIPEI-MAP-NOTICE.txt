The accompanying map-data/taipei_xinyi_buildings.json is the complete
machine-readable derived database used to generate the mapped Taipei buildings
and arterial streets. The source map and this derived database are available
under the Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL), version 1.0.

The central Xinyi extract uses OpenStreetMap snapshots from November 19, 2026.
It contains building outlines, building parts and major road centerlines within
a 4 km square around the Taipei 101 spire. The separate northern skyline context
uses a July 15, 2026 snapshot and includes mapped buildings with an explicit
height of at least 35 m or at least 11 above-ground floors. Later central
features take priority wherever the extracts overlap.

Source data were retrieved through the public Overpass API services at
https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter and
https://overpass.kumi.systems/api/interpreter. The database records the UTC
source timestamps and SHA-256 fingerprints of the responses.

The extract projects WGS84 coordinates to a local azimuthal equidistant plane
with origin 25.033668 N, 121.564810 E. Coordinates are metres east and north.
Polygons retain exterior and courtyard rings. Outlines are simplified by up to
0.75 m before coordinates are rounded to decimetres. Roads are clipped to the
central area. The compact output retains original OSM way/relation identifiers;
a suffix identifies separate connected pieces of the same element.

height_m and min_height_m preserve the mapped vertical extents in metres when
present. levels and min_level preserve mapped above-ground storey information.
Null means the source supplies no usable value. An OSM height tag may itself
be an estimate, and converting floors to metres in a rendered model also
produces an estimate. parent_id is a derived containment match between a part
and an enclosing building. It can be absent when no outline fully contains it.
The skyline selection, night lighting, materials and effects are artistic.
